Health & Disease Analytics

A web-based exploratory data analysis dashboard for public health data quality and disease pattern analysis in Malawi.

  • 1Check completeness, timeliness, consistency, validity and outliers in one place.
  • 2Explore disease trends over time and compare Malawi districts before drawing conclusions.
  • 3Aligned with Malawi 2063 — digital transformation and evidence-based health management.
